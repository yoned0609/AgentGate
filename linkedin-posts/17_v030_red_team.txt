I tried to kill my own product with AI. It won.

I built a full-LLM competitor to attack AgentGate's market position.
3 AI agents. 19 test scenarios. Sarcasm, slang, double negatives.

The LLM approach promised "deeper understanding."

Here's what actually happened:

AgentGate (regex)    → 100% accuracy, 0.16ms, $0/request
LLM competitor       →  94% accuracy, 721ms, $0.006/request

4,427x slower. Infinite cost ratio. And less accurate.

Why?
Authorization is a binary gate. Allow or deny.
HTTP method IS the ground truth — not the user's feelings about it.
A DELETE is a delete whether you say "nuke it" or "please remove."

But the exercise revealed real gaps.
So I fixed them all in v0.3.0:

→ 10 providers (was 3)
  Google, Microsoft, Slack, GitHub, Jira,
  Notion, Linear, HubSpot, Salesforce, AWS

→ Workflow-level authorization (NEW)
  Detects cross-provider data exfiltration.
  read on Google → create on Slack = blocked.

→ L3 async escalation
  Low-confidence intents fire webhooks for human review.
  Never blocks the <5ms deterministic path.

→ 6 analytics endpoints
  Time series. Deny trends. Anomaly detection.
  Latency percentiles. Intent distribution. Agent heatmaps.

→ Natural language policy builder
  "Agents can read issues but cannot delete repos"
  → Valid YAML policy, instantly loaded.

277 tests. Zero regressions.
